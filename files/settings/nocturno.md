## Instrucción técnica
```text
Convert this sketch/concept into a black-and-white analog photograph in the style of staged nocturnal flash photography.

LIGHTING:
Light the scene almost exclusively with a single direct on-camera flash. The subject(s) must be sharply and frontally lit, fully exposed, with hard-edged shadows and minimal falloff gradient. Everything more than a few meters from the subject must fall into pure, textured black — no ambient light, no visible horizon line, no gradual fade into darkness; the transition from lit subject to black background should be abrupt.

Alternative lighting mode (only if the concept calls for a "stage" moment): place a single fixed light source within the scene (lamp, spotlight, clearing) illuminating a background element — animal, object, patch of ground — while foreground figures remain in solid black silhouette, backlit and featureless.

COMPOSITION:
Human figure(s) isolated in empty black space, no ground line, no horizon, no spatial reference. Movement or dynamic poses allowed (dancing, reaching, turning), but camera placement stays deliberate and centered, never candid. Sense of a "stage" or clearing, not an open landscape.

ATMOSPHERE:
If animals appear: cats loose in the scene at night, silent witnesses — never stylized as fantastical or glowing.

FILM LOOK:
Black and white, visible analog film grain (high-ISO look), deep but textured blacks, no color tint, no digital smoothness or bokeh — handheld 35mm with on-camera flash in near-total darkness.

Do not add color. Do not soften flash into studio gradient. Do not add horizon or ambient moonlight glow.
```
