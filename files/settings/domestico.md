## Instrucción técnica
```text
Convert this sketch/concept into a black-and-white analog photograph in the style of staged domestic flash portraiture.

LIGHTING:
Single direct on-camera flash as only light source. Subject sharply and frontally lit, fully exposed, hard-edged shadows, minimal falloff gradient. Walls and background read as flat, evenly lit planes close to the subject — no soft ambient glow, no colored light, no studio diffusion.

COMPOSITION:
Subject frontal and symmetrical — seated or standing, centered or slightly off-center, looking straight at camera. Surrounding objects (furniture, shelving, screens, doorways) in rigid grid-like static composition, like an old family snapshot, not a styled photoshoot. Eye-level camera, roughly normal lens. Generous negative space of flat wall or shadow around subject.

FILM LOOK:
Black and white, visible analog film grain (high-ISO look), deep but textured blacks, no color tint, no digital smoothness or bokeh — handheld 35mm with on-camera flash, documentary interior photography tradition.

Do not add color. Do not soften flash into a studio gradient.
```
